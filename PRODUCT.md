# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Young adults using Nexus to discover and connect with new people nearby. The app ships in two flavors from one codebase: the main `nexus` flavor is open to any email domain and general-audience, while the `mec` flavor gates signup to a campus email domain for a student-only context. Same product, same design language, one variant scoped to a campus community. Users open the app in casual, in-the-moment moments (between classes, at an event, killing time) rather than in a heads-down planning session.

## Product Purpose

Nexus is a general social discovery app: browse/discover people (the "orbit" screen), like, match, and chat, with music-taste signal via Spotify integration. It is not framed as strictly romantic - discovery is broad (friends, connections, romance) and campus-scoped for the MEC flavor. Success looks like users feeling safe enough to actually meet people they discover in the app, which is why safety is core product surface, not a footnote: a Safety Center, meetup-safety guidance, and crisis helplines all live as first-class settings pages.

## Brand Personality

Playful & energetic, in a BeReal-style authentic/spontaneous register: casual, in-the-moment, unpolished-on-purpose rather than curated-perfect. Energy comes from genuine spontaneity, not from swipe-mechanic gamification.

## Anti-references

- Generic Tinder-style stacked swipe-deck cards - avoid the exact swipe-card visual cliché even where like/pass interactions exist.
- Curated-perfect, overly-polished dating-app gloss that undercuts the "authentic/spontaneous" feel.

## Design Principles

- **Safety is first-class, not a footnote.** The Safety Center, meetup-safety guidance, and crisis helplines should read as considered, well-designed product surfaces - never an afterthought settings dump.
- **Authenticity over gloss.** Favor spontaneous, in-the-moment, human textures over curated-perfect dating-app polish; energy comes from genuineness, not from swipe-mechanic gamification or engagement-bait patterns.
- **One product, two contexts.** The `nexus` and `mec` flavors share one design language; campus-gating is a data/config difference, not a reason to fork the visual system.
- **Discovery is broad, not romance-only.** Avoid visual conventions (swipe-deck cards, "match" fireworks, etc.) that hard-code a strictly-romantic framing onto a general social-discovery product.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA at minimum, leaning toward AAA-level contrast and motion care specifically around safety-sensitive surfaces (Safety Center, meetup-safety, crisis helplines) - that content needs to be legible and calm under stress, not just technically compliant. Reduced-motion alternatives are required everywhere, not optional polish.
