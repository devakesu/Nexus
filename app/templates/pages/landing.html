Questions answered.

No corporate vagueness. Clear answers about how Nexus actually works.

How do I install Nexus from Google Play?
For verified campus students, search Nexus MEC on Google Play or use package ID com.devakesu.apps.nexus.mec. It's live and available now. The general variant (com.devakesu.apps.nexus) is currently in pre-launch and coming soon.
How does the Meetup Check-in Timer work?
Before meeting someone in person, arm a Check-in Timer (e.g. 30 minutes) from Safety Center. You get a unique PIN. If you don't extend or disarm the timer before it expires, Nexus automatically sends emergency alerts and a live Web Portal link to your designated Emergency Contacts.
What's the difference between Nexus and Nexus MEC?
Both share the same codebase, features, and design language. The general variant (nexus) opens discovery to any email domain. The MEC variant (nexus_mec) restricts signup to verified @mec.ac.in campus student email domains - ensuring the orbit is strictly your campus community.
Are my messages really end-to-end encrypted?
Yes. Nexus uses an asymmetric ECDH prekey bundle exchange (similar to Signal Protocol) to set up a per-session E2EE tunnel for every conversation. Messages are encrypted on your device before leaving it, and only decrypted on the recipient's device. The backend server only stores and relays encrypted ciphertext - it never has access to plaintext.
Can I export or delete my account data?
Yes to both. Data export gives you a full JSON archive of your account, settings, and chat metadata. Account deletion requests trigger a 30-day grace period before permanent, irreversible data purging - so you can change your mind.
What is Ghost Mode and how does it work?
Ghost Mode is a single-toggle privacy control accessible from your profile. When active, your orbit node is completely hidden from all nearby discovery radars - you won't appear in anyone's orbit. Your existing matched chats remain accessible and functional. Ghost Mode doesn't log you out or delete data.
