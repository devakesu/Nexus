What is Nexus?
Nexus brings three ways to connect into one app — Dating, Friends, and Professional — all built around a single profile. Switch tabs any time; who you meet changes, but your profile stays yours.
How do I set up my profile?
During onboarding you'll pick your display name, age, and demographic bucket (for the main Nexus app) or your major/branch and study year (for Nexus MEC). You'll also verify your phone number with an OTP. After onboarding, you can fill in your bio, location, lifestyle details, interests, and linked Spotify artists from the Profile tab at any time.
Can Dating, Friends, and Professional all be active at once?
Yes. Each context has its own activation toggle, so you can be discoverable for Dating and Professional networking at the same time, or focus on just one — entirely up to you.
What's Nexus MEC?
Nexus MEC is our campus-community flavor. It verifies you with a campus Google Workspace email (only approved institutional domains can sign in) and lets you export your profile to the main Nexus app via a one-time code — built for closed university communities.
How do I move my profile from Nexus MEC to the main Nexus app?
From your Nexus MEC Profile tab, tap the Export Profile Data card to generate a 6-character code — it's valid for 15 minutes and works only once. Enter that code during onboarding in the main Nexus app to pull your profile across.
What is a Demographic Bucket?
On the main Nexus app, you pick a demographic bucket during onboarding — it's your primary community identification (e.g. Men, Women, Non-Binary). This helps surface you to the right people without exposing personal details. You can update it later from your Profile tab.

What is Orbit?
Orbit is our discovery screen. Instead of a flat swipe deck, nearby profiles appear as interactive nodes arranged in concentric tiers around you on a 2D spatial radar canvas — positioned by match closeness and real proximity vectors.
How do Orbit Tiers work?
Profiles appear across 3 concentric orbital rings: Tier 1 (Immediate - 50m), Tier 2 (Block - 250m), and Tier 3 (Neighbourhood - 500m). Node size and glow intensity reflect overall match compatibility.
Can I filter my Orbit view?
Yes. Tap the filter icon on the Orbit screen to adjust maximum distance (up to 500m), age ranges, minimum Spotify vibe match ratio, or specific interests. Filters apply instantly without reloading.
How do I pause or hide my profile from Orbit?
Toggle off Dating, Friends, or Professional in your Profile tab or enable Ghost Mode. When Ghost Mode is active, your node vanishes from all nearby radars while your existing matches and chats remain untouched.

What is a Safety Check-in?
A Safety Check-in is a countdown timer you arm before meeting someone in person. If you don't enter your secret PIN to extend or disarm the timer before it expires, Nexus automatically dispatches emergency alerts and a live Web Portal link to your designated Emergency Contacts.
Who receives my Safety Check-in alerts?
Only the Emergency Contacts you explicitly designate in Settings → Safety Center → Emergency Contacts. They receive an SMS and email with your meetup location notes and a link to your live Safety Web Portal.
What happens if I forget my disarm PIN?
If an incorrect PIN is entered 3 times or the timer runs out, the emergency alert sequence triggers automatically. You can also request a PIN reset via email OTP if you still have active phone connectivity.

How does end-to-end encryption work on Nexus?
Nexus uses an asymmetric ECDH prekey bundle exchange (Signal Protocol architecture). Every message and voice note is encrypted directly on your device before transmission. The backend server stores and relays ciphertext only — it never has access to plaintext message contents.
Are voice notes and photos also encrypted?
Yes. Media attachments and voice notes are encrypted symmetrically using per-message AES keys before being uploaded. The key is transmitted inside the E2EE signal payload, so only the intended recipient can decrypt the file.
