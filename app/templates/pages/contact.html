Frequently Asked Questions

How do account suspension appeals work?

If your account was suspended or restricted, select 'Suspended Account' as your topic. Submit your registered email or phone number along with details regarding your appeal. Our safety team reviews every appeal within 24-48 hours.

Why is email OTP verification required?

Email OTP verification prevents spam and ensures that ticket update notifications reach a valid, accessible inbox.

How can I track my ticket status?

Log into the Nexus Mobile App and visit Settings → Help, Feedback & Bug Report to view ticket progress, read staff comments, or add follow-up details.
