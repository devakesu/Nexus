Account Lifecycle Protocol (3 Steps)

When you submit an account deletion request, your profile is instantly signed out on all active devices and hidden from discovery. You are granted a {{ grace_period_days }}-day grace period to restore your account before permanent, non-reversible anonymization takes effect.

Recommended Step

Backup Your Account Data

Before requesting account deletion, we strongly recommend exporting a full, machine-readable copy of your personal data (JSON format). Once the {{ grace_period_days }}-day grace window expires, this data is permanently purged and cannot be recovered.

What's Included
  • • Profile details & account settings
  • • Account login history & active devices
  • • Matches & discovery history
  • • Encrypted chat metadata & timestamps
  • • Support tickets & feedback history
  • • Safety sessions & connected Spotify playlists
What's Excluded
  • • Chat message contents (E2E encrypted, never on servers)
  • • Safety recording decryption keys
  • • Internal security telemetry & moderation notes
SECURITY NOTICE: Store your exported data file securely. Never share your export JSON with untrusted parties. Nexus is not liable for data exposed from personal storage.

Immediate Deactivation Actions

Step 1 of 3: Day 0
  • You will be immediately signed out of your account on all web and mobile devices.
  • Your profile and media will no longer be visible to other members on Orbit discovery.
  • All active matches and active conversation threads will be closed. Other users will see that you are no longer available.
  • You will cease to receive email notifications, push updates, or match recommendations.

Within {{ grace_period_days }} Days (Restoration Window)

Step 2 of 3: Days 1–{{ grace_period_days }}

We understand mindsets change. Your data remains in a dormant state for {{ grace_period_days }} calendar days:

  • Logging back into the Nexus app or web portal at any time before the {{ grace_period_days }} days elapse automatically presents an option to Cancel Deletion.
  • Canceling deletion instantly restores your profile details, photo grid, active matches, and historical conversation threads.

After {{ grace_period_days }} Days (Permanent Anonymization)

Step 3 of 3: Day {{ grace_period_days }}+ (Tier-1 Purge)
  • Your name, photos, bio details, and profile attributes are irreversibly anonymized from our production databases.
    • {% if blocklist_cooldown_days > 0 %}
  • If your account was suspended or flagged for severe safety violations, a secure one-way HMAC hash of your phone number is retained for {{ blocklist_cooldown_days }} days to prevent rapid re-registration. Otherwise, your phone hash is removed immediately.
    • {% endif %}
  • This action is permanent and non-reversible beyond the {{ grace_period_days }}-day window.

Data Retention & Statutory Holds

Tier-2 Final Hard Purge (After {{ long_tail_purge_formatted }})

Under DPDP §13, GDPR Art. 17(3), and applicable statutory requirements, certain anonymized records are retained strictly for safety and legal defense before undergoing Tier-2 hard purge:

  • Historical safety reports and moderation audit logs are retained anonymously to preserve platform trust and prevent repeat offenders.
  • Meetup Safety alerts are retained for {{ safety_data_legal_hold_days }} days and Digital Witness session metadata is retained for {{ safety_evidence_active_retention_days }} days to assist active law enforcement inquiries.
  • Tax, financial ledger records, and legal compliance logs required by law will be archived separately.
  • Tier-2 Hard Purge: All retained compliance records and system database rows are permanently hard-purged after {{ long_tail_purge_days }} days ({{ long_tail_purge_formatted }}). No personal data is stored indefinitely.
